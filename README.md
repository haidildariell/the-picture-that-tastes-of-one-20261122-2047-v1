Bounty: [The picture that tastes of one ingredient](https://make.fun/bounty/the-picture-that-tastes-of-one-20261122-2047) on make.fun.

One ingredient, nothing else: raw honey. An extreme macro of natural honeycomb brimming with thick golden honey, one slow ribbon of it dripping and pooling in warm sunlight — the whole picture is the taste of honey. `main.py` simply copies the bundled `asset.jpg` photograph to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte, so the entry is identical on every run.
