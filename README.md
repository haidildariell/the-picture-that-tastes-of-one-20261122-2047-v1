Bounty: [The picture that tastes of one ingredient](https://make.fun/bounty/the-picture-that-tastes-of-one-20261122-2047)

One ingredient, nothing else: an extreme macro of natural honeycomb brimming with golden honey, a translucent ribbon of it dripping down and pooling in warm sunlight. The program ships the photograph as `asset.jpg` and `main.py` copies its bytes, unchanged, to the output `entry.png` on every run.
